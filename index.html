Driver

I work as an IT Developer at Spindo. I build the internal tools other teams use: an asset inventory, a reporting dashboard, and now an access link register that has to keep secrets from its own administrators.

I learn by building the thing. Flask and PostgreSQL started as a practice CRUD app. The same stack now runs ALR, with migrations, tests, and an audit log nobody can edit.

Before that: Laravel sites, Flutter apps, and a thesis about stock-keeping for small grocery stores. Three to five years of writing code. Nobody here is pretending it has been ten.
